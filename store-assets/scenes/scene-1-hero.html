# tf–idf

In [information retrieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/…),
**tf–idf** is a measure of importance of a word
to a document in a collection, adjusted for the
fact that some words appear more frequently.

## Term frequency

Term frequency, tf(*t*,*d*), is the relative
frequency of term *t* within document *d*,

$\mathrm{tf}(t,d) = \frac{f_{t,d}}{\sum_{t' \in d} f_{t',d}}$

| weighting scheme | tf weight |
| --- | --- |
| binary | 0, 1 |
| raw count | $f_{t,d}$ |
| log normalization | $\log(1 + f_{t,d})$ |
