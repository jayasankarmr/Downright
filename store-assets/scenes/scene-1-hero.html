# tf–idf

In [information retrieval](https://en.wikipedia…),
**tf–idf** is a measure of importance of a
word to a document in a collection, adjusted
for the fact that some words appear more
frequently.

## Term frequency

Term frequency, tf(*t*,*d*), is the relative
frequency of term *t* within document *d*,

$\mathrm{tf}(t,d) = f_{t,d} / \sum f_{t',d}$

| weighting scheme  | tf weight        |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| binary            | 0, 1             |
| raw count         | $f_{t,d}$        |
