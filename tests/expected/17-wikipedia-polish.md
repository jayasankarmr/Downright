# Battle of the Example Sea

| Battle of the Example Sea |  |
| --- | --- |
| Date · 4–8 May 1942<br>Location · Example Sea |  |
| Result | Tactical draw |

The battle was the first action in which the opposing fleets neither saw nor fired directly upon one another, a milestone widely covered in later histories of the war and still cited in modern naval doctrine.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/carrier.jpg?lang=en)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Carrier.jpg)

*[Shokaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_aircraft_carrier_Shokaku)* under attack by dive bombers during the battle

The carrier ***[Lexington](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Lexington_(CV-2)>)*** was lost, a setback the ***Allies*** could absorb. Reports reached [Pearl Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Harbor) within the day.
