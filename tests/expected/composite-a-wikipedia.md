A **rocket engine** uses stored propellants as the reaction mass for forming a high-speed propulsive jet of fluid.<sup>[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_engine#cite_note-1)</sup> Newton's third law gives $F=ma$ as the governing relation for every propulsion system of this kind.

## History

Early engines were developed for [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft) use, then adapted for launch vehicles during the space race, growing rapidly in both scale and reliability.

## Principles

The exhaust velocity relation is $$\Delta v=v_{e}\ln {\frac {m_{0}}{m_{f}}}$$ for an ideal rocket in field-free space.
