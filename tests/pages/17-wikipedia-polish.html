Battle of the Example Sea

Battle of the Example Sea
Date4–8 May 1942
LocationExample Sea
ResultTactical draw

The battle was the first action in which the opposing fleets neither saw nor fired directly upon one another, a milestone widely covered in later histories of the war and still cited in modern naval doctrine.

The carrier Lexington was lost, a setback the Allies could absorb. Reports reached Pearl Harbor within the day.
