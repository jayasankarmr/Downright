A rocket engine uses stored propellants as the reaction mass for forming a high-speed propulsive jet of fluid.[1] Newton's third law gives as the governing relation for every propulsion system of this kind.

Historyedit

Early engines were developed for aircraft use, then adapted for launch vehicles during the space race, growing rapidly in both scale and reliability.
