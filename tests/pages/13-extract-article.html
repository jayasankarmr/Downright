How to Grow Tomatoes

By Sam Gardner

Tomatoes need full sun, deep watering, and steady feeding to produce well. Choose a spot that gets at least eight hours of direct light, work compost into the bed before planting, and set supports early so the roots are never disturbed later in the season.

Water deeply twice a week rather than a little every day, and mulch generously to keep the moisture even. Pinch out the side shoots that appear between the stem and the branches, and feed with a high-potash fertiliser once the first truss has set fruit.
