Privacy

The short version, which is also the whole version.

Downright makes no network requests. Not for fonts, not for analytics, not for updates to anything but the browser’s own extension update check. Nothing you clip leaves this browser. Settings ride along with your browser profile sync and nowhere else.

That claim is checked mechanically on every commit, and the source is public if you would rather check it yourself.
