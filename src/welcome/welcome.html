Downright is ready

Any page → downright correct Markdown, on your clipboard.

  1. Press Alt+M (Option+M on Mac) on any page — the article lands on your clipboard as clean Markdown, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Obsidian, or a doc.
  2. Select text first and the same keystroke — or right-click → “Copy selection as Markdown” — clips just the selection, keeping lists, tables, and code intact.
  3. Click the toolbar icon to preview the Markdown before copying, switch between Article and Full-page capture, or save the clip as a .md file.

The Downright promise

Tables come out as real tables. Code fences keep their language. LaTeX survives. Links and images become absolute URLs. And none of it ever leaves your browser — Downright makes no network requests, ever, and can’t read any page until the moment you invoke it.

Change the shortcut or defaults any time in the extension’s settings.
